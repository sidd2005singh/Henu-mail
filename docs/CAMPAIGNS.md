## 1. 8-Step Campaign Pipeline
1. **Identifier**: Name & internal description.
2. **Audience Screening**: Real-time partition (`Recipients`, `Suppressed`, `Invalid`, `Ready`).
3. **Sender Rotation**: Multi-account allocation.
4. **Sequence Builder**: Step-based delays (`Email 1` $\rightarrow$ Wait 3d $\rightarrow$ `Email 2` $\rightarrow$ Wait 4d $\rightarrow$ `Email 3` $\rightarrow$ `STOP`).
5. **Personalization Inspector**: Real-time preview with Prev/Next controls across actual audience records.
6. **Schedule & Timezone Windows**: Strict sending hours (e.g. Mon-Fri 09:00 - 18:00).
7. **Pre-Flight Safety Check**: 8-point pass/fail validation.
8. **Launch**: Real-time queue engagement.
